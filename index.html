On the menu

Chippy classics, and then some.

Fish and chips is the main event. There are kebabs, burgers and pies too, plus meal deals with a can of pop.

1Fish & chips

Fish in golden batter with a proper portion of chip shop chips. Add mushy peas.

2Station Specials

Meal deals with chips and a can of pop: small fish, doner meat, chicken or battered halloumi.

3Kebabs & burgers

Doner kebabs and burgers, with chips on the side and your choice of sauce.

4Pies

Pukka pies, served hot. Have one with chips.

Full menu and prices are on our ordering apps.

The shop

A local chippy on Haslucks Green Road.

Station Fish Bar is a takeaway in Shirley, Solihull. Come in and order at the counter, ring ahead and collect, or have it delivered through Just Eat, Deliveroo or Uber Eats.

We’re open for lunch and again in the evening, Monday to Saturday, and on Sunday evenings too.
